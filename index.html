About VISTECH

Varsity Institute of Science and Technology (Pty) LTD

Varsity Institute of Science and Technology (Pty) LTD (VISTECH) is a multicultural and dynamic Institution which is proudly South African. The phenomenal success story of the college is one that has surprised critics and won over sceptics.

Since its establishment in 2015, and under the bold and visionary leadership of its Director of Studies (D.O.S) Dennis Nduka Allison, the institution has inspired the community by transforming and serving humanity through innovation and the collaborative pursuit of knowledge. We are a reputable, comprehensive, flexible, and accessible institution motivating a future generation as the African college shaping futures in the service of humanity.

Our Vision

To be South Africa's leading higher education institution, empowering students through science and technology, as well as highly skilled graduates that have the best chance of succeeding in the job market.

Our Mission & Philosophy

To provide an educational experience of the highest calibre with a strong focus on team commitment and student success. It's time to build a dream: “A World Class Institution.” We believe that we do not succeed until our students succeed.
